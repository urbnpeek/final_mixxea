How to book

01

Pick the artist

Use the roster below or the artist directory. Label-only profiles stay on the label side and do not show a book button.

02

Send the hold

Include the date, city, venue, and the offer details you already have. A short note is enough to open the request.

03

Freq Vault replies

The reply goes to the email on the inquiry. Availability and terms are confirmed from there.

Questions

How do I book an artist?

Email booking@mixxea.com or use the form. Include the artist, date, city, venue, and the offer details you already have.

Which artists can I request?

The roster on this page is the same artist list as the Mixxea homepage and the artist directory.

Is Freq Vault separate from Mixxea?

Freq Vault is the booking agency. Mixxea is the record label. Both are on mixxea.com. The label page stays at Record Label.

Where do demos go?

Demos use the A&R path at Submit Demo. The booking form is for promoters and event requests.
