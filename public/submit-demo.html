Submit a Demo to Mixxea A&R

Demos

Submission form

Private download link, profile, and a short note. Or email ar@mixxea.com.

What Mixxea A&R listens for

Electronic club music, sent as a private link with a short note on the track.

The music

Techno, house, ambient, bass, and leftfield club music — the same ground as the Mixxea catalogue.

Unreleased tracks

The form asks if the track has been released. Unreleased, exclusive music is preferred.

A note on the track

Add a short description: the mood, where it sits, and what you want a listener to hear.

How to submit

The submission form is four steps. It goes to Mixxea A&R.

01

Open the form

Artist name, email, country, and a Spotify, SoundCloud, or other profile link.

02

Add the track

Title, genre, BPM, and a private download link — WeTransfer, Dropbox, or Google Drive.

03

Send it

Writers, samples, and your confirmation. Send a download link. The form does not take a file upload.

What happens next

Sending a demo does not promise a release.

A&R reads the form

The submission goes to Mixxea A&R. A track is not released or distributed without a signed agreement.

Bookings are separate

Shows, holds, and management inquiries use the booking agency, not the demo form.
