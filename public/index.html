01 · The Label

MIXXEA
RECORDS

Mixxea Records is a global electronic music label built for artists who refuse to compromise. We develop careers, not just catalogues — signing artists who have something real to say with sound.

From deep techno to hypnotic house, abstract ambient to experimental bass — we operate at the bleeding edge of electronic culture, with distribution across every major market and a network built over years in the industry.

Booking Agency & Artist Management

Whether you need a headline act for your festival, a resident for your club series, or full artist management — FreqVault is the bridge between talent and opportunity.
